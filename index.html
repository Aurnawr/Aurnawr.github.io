B-DENSE: Branching for Dense Ensemble Network Supervision Efficiency

Supervise a distilled diffusion student on the teacher's full denoising trajectory, recovering the intermediate steps that standard distillation throws away.

FID 39.66 → 20.81 vs. Progressive Distillation · CIFAR-10, 128 steps
FID 10.25 → 9.57 vs. SFD · ImageNet-64, NFE 2

Paper Code

The student gets K-fold branched output channels (K·C instead of C), so each branch is supervised against a different point on the teacher's trajectory. The gains above cost roughly 0.01% extra FLOPs during training, with identical inference cost.

PyTorch · diffusers · Optuna · Lightning.ai (A100 / L4)

NeurIPS 2026 · VLM4RWD WorkshopVLM hallucination · reproducibility

Rethinking the Effectiveness of Contrastive Decoding in Mitigating Hallucinations in MLLMs

Contrastive decoding's hallucination gains are a distributional artifact, not corrected perception.

Indistinguishable from non-visual controls on precision and hallucination rate;
the whole effect is one recall shift (+12.21 pp, p < 0.001)

Paper Code

A reproducibility study stress-testing VCD, ICD and SID across LLaVA-1.5 (7B / 13B) and Qwen2.5-VL on POPE, MME and CHAIR, against spurious, non-visual controls. A logit-lens analysis localizes the layers where the intervention breaks down.

PyTorch · HuggingFace · logit lens

VLM-Reliability: Modality Gaps & Vision Refusal

Why do VLMs refuse harmful text but not harmful images? Text- and image-side refusal directions in LLaVA-1.5-7B barely align — cosine similarity never exceeds 0.3 — and the text side consistently dominates in norm.

Code

Refusal directions are extracted from residual-stream activations for each modality and compared geometrically, identifying the modality gap as a primary driver of weak vision-side refusal.

PyTorch · HuggingFace · mechanistic probing

DataReasoningMay 2026

Visual-CoT

A resumable pipeline that turns raw ChartQA samples into a fine-tuning corpus of structured visual chain-of-thought traces — summary → extraction → computation → answer.

5 verification gates · 3 teacher samples per item · 95% yield

Code

Three traces per item are sampled from a frontier VLM teacher and filtered through schema, answer-correctness, numeric-grounding, arithmetic re-verification and 2-of-3 self-consistency gates; the most grounded survivor is kept.

Python · agentic workflow · VLM API
